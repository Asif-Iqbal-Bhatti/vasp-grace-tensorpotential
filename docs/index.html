GRACE-1L · 2L · 3L foundation models · ASE · VASP file formats

Your VASP inputs, run on a machine-learning potential.

vasp-grace reads POSCAR and INCAR, computes energies, forces and stresses with a GRACE model, and writes OUTCAR, OSZICAR, CONTCAR and XDATCAR the way VASP does. Ten command-line tools then take those results into defects, ion transport and topological phonons.

VASP drop-in

Same files in, same files out

An existing INCAR works unchanged. vasp-grace reads the tags it understands, adds one of its own (GRACE_MODEL), and ignores the rest, so scripts that parse VASP output keep working.

Start with the 2L family

GRACE-2L-OAM is the default and lines up with Materials Project–style PBE data. GRACE-2L-OMAT is fitted on OMat24 alone. The newer GRACE-2L-OMAT-medium/large and GRACE-2L-SMAX models add built-in uncertainty.

Trade accuracy for speed

Two-layer models are semi-local and more accurate. Single-layer 1L and FS models are faster for long MD on large cells. Use the -fp64 variants for phonons and elastic constants, where finite differences amplify numerical noise.

Uncertainty from a single model

UQ-capable models report CALM uncertainty: a per-atom extrapolation grade γ from the Mahalanobis distance of each atom's descriptor to the training-set clusters. γ above 1 means the model is extrapolating. grace-active-learning --calm and grace-mc --calm use it to flag structures for DFT, with no committee needed.

Ten commands

What you can compute

Every command takes --model as a foundation-model name or a path to your own model, and writes plain-text data files next to publication-ready plots.

vasp-grace

The VASP drop-in

Driven entirely by INCAR. Writes VASP-format output at every ionic step.

  • Single points with energy, forces and full stress tensor
  • Relaxation of positions, cell, or cell shape at fixed volume
  • NVE, NVT (Andersen, Berendsen, Langevin) and NPT molecular dynamics, written to XDATCAR
  • Phonon band structure and DOS from finite displacements
  • Full elastic tensor Cij and Birch coefficients

Structure and uncertainty

grace-active-learning

Scores structures by CALM uncertainty from one UQ-capable model, or by how much a committee of models disagrees, and flags those that need a DFT label.

uncertainty_log.dat · flagged/POSCAR_*
grace-mc

Metropolis Monte Carlo with displacement and species-swap moves, for site disorder such as Cl/S in argyrodites. Flags extrapolated snapshots on the fly with CALM or a committee.

MC_energies.dat · XDATCAR_MC
grace-dislocation

Edge, screw and mixed dislocations from the isotropic Volterra field or the anisotropic Stroh solution, as dipoles or single cores, with optional GRACE relaxation.

POSCAR_dislocation · dislocation_info.txt

Ion dynamics and transport

grace-lihopping

Detects ion hops between lattice sites with a sojourn-time filter that ignores rattling, builds the hop network, separates grain-boundary from bulk hops, and fits an Arrhenius activation energy.

hop_events.dat · hop_network.dat · arrhenius.png
grace-kappa

Lattice thermal conductivity κ from Müller-Plathe reverse non-equilibrium MD. Needs no per-atom stress, so it works with any potential.

kappa_summary.txt · temperature_profile.png

Condensed matter and topology

grace-topo-phonons

Finds phonon band crossings that are candidate Weyl, Dirac or nodal-line points, and computes the Berry phase on a loop around each one.

phonon_crossings.dat · phonon_topo_bands.png
grace-phonon-berry

Band-resolved phonon Zak phases along a closed k-path, plus Berry curvature Ω(k) on a 2D mesh integrated to Chern numbers.

phonon_zak.dat · phonon_chern.png
grace-topology

Persistent homology of the atomic structure. Betti curves β₀, β₁, β₂ give a fingerprint that needs no symmetry, for grain boundaries and amorphous regions.

betti_curves.dat · persistence_diagram.png
grace-moire

Commensurate twisted bilayers by the coincidence-site-lattice method, angle scans, GRACE relaxation and AA/AB/SP stacking maps.

POSCAR_moire · stacking_map.png

Worked pipelines

The tools feed each other

Outputs from one command are inputs to the next, so a full study runs on one potential without leaving the package.

Anisotropic dislocation core

  1. Fit the elastic tensor: IBRION = 6, ISIF = 3 in vasp-grace
  2. Build the Stroh displacement field from it: grace-dislocation --method anisotropic --elastic ELASTIC_Cij_GPa.dat
  3. Relax the core with the same model: add --relax

Fine-tuning by active learning

  1. Sample configurations with MD (IBRION = 0) or grace-mc
  2. Score them with CALM uncertainty: grace-active-learning --xdatcar XDATCAR --calm GRACE-2L-OMAT-medium-ft-AM
  3. Label flagged/POSCAR_* with DFT and fine-tune GRACE on them

Ionic conductivity of an electrolyte

  1. Run NVT MD at 600, 800 and 1000 K with vasp-grace
  2. Extract hop rates per temperature: grace-lihopping --arrhenius --temps 600 800 1000
  3. Read the activation energy Eₐ and compare grain boundary against bulk

Topological phonons

  1. Compute force constants with IBRION = 6; they are cached in phonon.*.json
  2. Locate crossings and their Berry phase: grace-topo-phonons --berry
  3. Classify bands with Zak phases and Chern numbers: grace-phonon-berry --curvature

Scope

What a potential can tell you

GRACE models the Born–Oppenheimer energy surface. Anything that follows from the motion of the nuclei is in reach. Anything that needs the electrons is not.

In reach

  • Structures, relaxations, equations of state
  • Phonons, elastic constants, lattice instabilities
  • Diffusion, hopping barriers, thermal conductivity
  • Defects, dislocations, grain boundaries, twisted bilayers
  • Topology of phonon bands and of the atomic structure

Hand off to DFT

  • Electronic bands, Z₂ and Chern invariants of electrons
  • Electron–phonon coupling λ and superconducting Tc
  • Magnetic order and spin textures
  • Energy differences below a few meV/atom without fine-tuning

For these, use vasp-grace to screen and pre-relax structures cheaply, then pass them to VASP, Wannier90 or EPW.
